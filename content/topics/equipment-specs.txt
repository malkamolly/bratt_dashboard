@welcome
eyebrow: How To Use This
title: Two kinds of numbers.
subtitle: Every machine has both — mixing them up is how a truck shows up and can't do the job.
body: **Drive dimensions** (length, width, height, weight) tell you whether the machine can *get to* the tree — through the gate, down the driveway, over the ground.
body: **Working dimensions** (outrigger/stabilizer spread, working height, reach) tell you what it can *do* once it's parked and set up.
body: When you're sizing up a job, check both against the yard before you commit a machine to it.

@three-rules
eyebrow: Cranes · The Fleet
title: Three cranes, three footprints
subtitle: Pick the smallest one that can still make the picks.
rule: 20T · Manitex 22101 | The "Tree Monster." **Gets into driveways and yards** with less damage than the 40T — but it will still crack weak sidewalks and weak driveways.
rule: 40T · Manitex 40124SHL | **Designed to stay on the road.** The most capacity of the three, but it will crack sidewalks and driveways.
rule: KB · EFFER 505 6S+3S HD | Knuckle boom, also **built to stay on the road.** Runs a hook and slings or grapple-saw attachments — reach and capacity depend on the attachment.


@three-rules
eyebrow: Cranes · Setup Rules
title: Before you set up
subtitle: The same three checks apply on every crane job.
rule: Get the disclaimer signed | **Never drive onto private property without a signed Equipment Disclaimer.** All three cranes will crack weak concrete.
rule: Check the slope | Setting up over **3°** needs approval on the 20T and 40T; the KB needs a **second opinion over 4°.** Use the slope chart to turn a driveway's drop into a percentage.
rule: Measure from the turret | Reach is measured from the turret, not the bumper. **40T: 8′ from the rear.** 20T: confirm on site (sources say 4′ or 6′).
